Questions before you compare

Is the calculator free?

Yes. It runs in your browser and does not ask for an account or payment.

Where do my entries go?

They stay on your device. They are not sent to us, added to the page address or saved after you reload.

How is extra rent estimated?

A full monthly charge is counted for each renewal on or before the later of your finish date and your notice end date.

What if my facility prorates or refunds rent?

Ask for the final amount and enter it as the confirmed rent amount for that plan.

Does it find truck prices for me?

No. Bring your own quotes, including mileage, fuel, tax, coverage and help.

Are deposits and overdue balances included?

No. Deposits, overdue balances, disputed charges and future rate changes need a separate adjustment.

Can I rely on the result as my final bill?

No. It is an estimate from what you enter. Your agreement and the facility decide the final amount.
